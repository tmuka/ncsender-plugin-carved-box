Fit

The lip stands up round the opening of one half and slides into a widened opening in the other; they meet on the shoulder outside it.

Holding Tabs

The outlines are cut last, a little into the spoilboard, leaving tabs to hold the halves.

Magnets

Holes facing each other where the halves meet, the same in both. Glue the magnets in with opposite poles up, so they pull together.

Finger Notch

A scoop in the base's rim at the front, to lift the lid by.
